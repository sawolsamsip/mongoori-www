## The Basic Math: Gross vs Net Income

Let's start with the reality that trips don't equal take-home pay. Here's what most new drivers don't understand:

**Gross earnings** = What Uber/Lyft tells you that you made
**Net income** = What actually ends up in your bank account after expenses

Most new drivers focus on the gross number and get disappointed when their actual paycheck is much smaller.
