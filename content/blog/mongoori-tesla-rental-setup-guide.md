# Mongoori Tesla Rental Setup Guide: Your Complete First Month as an Irvine Driver

So you've decided to become a rideshare driver with a Tesla rental from Mongoori. Great choice. Now what?

If you're worried about the process—don't be. We've designed Mongoori specifically so that drivers can get approved, pick up their Tesla, and start driving within 48 hours. This guide walks you through every step.

## Before You Apply: What You'll Need

Mongoori requires a few things to process your application quickly:

**Required documents:**
- Valid California driver's license (minimum 3 years driving experience)
- Social Security Number (for background check)
- Proof of income or tax returns (last 2 years) — shows you can handle the responsibility
- Email address and phone number (for communication)
- Valid email for Uber/Lyft driver account

**Requirements:**
- Must be 21+ years old
- Clean driving record (minor infractions OK, DUIs/major violations disqualify)
- Active Uber/Lyft driver account (or willingness to set up one)
- Proof of California residency (utility bill or lease agreement)

**Time to prepare:** 15-20 minutes to gather documents

Mongoori doesn't require cash deposits or large down payments upfront—you pay through weekly earnings deductions. This makes it accessible to new drivers without startup capital.

## Step-by-Step: Application to First Drive (48 Hours)

1. Go to **[rides.mongoori.com](https://rides.mongoori.com)**

   - Personal info

   - Driver's license (front & back)

**What happens next:** Mongoori's driver ops team reviews applications. Approval typically takes 12-24 hours for straightforward applications.

### Day 2: Vehicle Pickup (1.5 hours)

**What to bring:**
- Driver's license
- Proof of insurance (if you have your own; Mongoori provides coverage)
- Phone (fully charged)
- Any questions written down

**The pickup process:**

1. **Arrive at your scheduled time.** Mongoori's facility is clean, professional, and organized. You'll check in with a staff member.

2. **Vehicle orientation (20 minutes).** A Mongoori specialist walks you through:
   - Tesla controls (very different from gas cars—more intuitive than you'd expect)

   - Charging locations and how to use the app

   - What to do if there's a problem (spoiler: call Mongoori, we handle it)

3. **Test drive (15 minutes).** You'll take a short drive to get comfortable with the Tesla:
   - Acceleration (smooth and instant—it's wild)
   - Regenerative braking (feels different but you'll love it)
   - Steering and handling

4. **Contract signing (15 minutes).** Digital signature on:
   - Rental agreement

   - Usage terms
   - Earning deduction terms (how rental costs come out of your Uber/Lyft earnings)

5. **First charging lesson (10 minutes).** Staff shows you:
   - How to charge at the facility
   - Home charging setup (if you have a Level 2 charger)
   - Public charging apps and stations
   - Expected charging times

6. **Vehicle handoff.** You receive:
   - Key fob (or keycard for newer Teslas)
   - Charging cable and adapter
   - Mongoori emergency contact card
   - First-week tips sheet

**Total time: 1.5 hours. You drive away with a fully charged Tesla Model 3 or Model Y.**

## Day 2-3: Setup and Preparation (Without the Car)

Before you log in to Uber/Lyft and start driving, set up your digital life:

### 1. Download Essential Apps (15 minutes)

**Must-have:**
- **Tesla app** — Check charge level, precondition (warm up) the car remotely, unlock if you forget your key
- **Uber Driver / Lyft Driver** — Your main income apps
- **Plugshare** — Find public charging stations near you
- **ChargePoint** — Another charging app (many stations accept both)
- **Google Maps** — Navigation (it's better than Tesla's for rideshare routes)

**Nice-to-have:**
- **Mongoori Driver Portal** — Track your rental payments, report issues, communicate with support

### 2. Set Up Your Charging Strategy (15 minutes)

**At home:**
- Plug in to a standard 120V outlet overnight (adds 2-3 miles/hour—slow but useful)
- Better option: Install a Level 2 charger (240V) for full charge in 8-10 hours—most Mongoori drivers do this
- Cost of installation: $500-1,200 (one-time)

**On the road:**
- Save 3-4 public charging stations near your usual driving area
- Plugshare shows real-time availability—use this
- Many are free (malls, grocery stores) or $2-4/charge
- Avoid getting below 20% battery if you're driving peak hours

**Pro tip:** Tesla's navigation automatically routes you through charging stations if your battery gets low. Trust it.

### 3. Prepare Your Car (10 minutes)

Before your first passenger:
- Clean the interior thoroughly (vacuum, wipe surfaces)
- Clean windows and mirrors
- Stock water bottles in cup holders
- Put phone mount on dashboard
- Test the charging cable setup

**First impression matters.** Clean Tesla = 5-star rating.

### 4. Phone Setup (10 minutes)

- Full brightness during day, auto-brightness at night
- Uber/Lyft app visible on home screen
- Emergency contacts (Mongoori, your family)
- Do NOT mount phone in driver's direct sightline—passenger comfort first

**Days 5-7 (15-20 more rides):**

**First week net profit:** $400-600 (after ~$100 rental cost)

### Common First-Week Questions & Answers

**"How much charge will I lose doing 8 hours of driving?"**
A full Tesla has 250-330 miles of range. Most drivers use 80-120 miles per 8-hour shift. You'll use 30-50% of your battery. Charge overnight to full.

**"Do I charge at the Mongoori facility or at home?"**
Either. Most drivers charge at home (cheaper, more convenient). Mongoori facility is backup if you need it mid-week.

**"What if I get a flat tire?"**
Call Mongoori immediately. We have roadside assistance. You don't pay extra. You're not stuck.

**"What if the car has a mechanical issue?"**
Call Mongoori. We handle all maintenance and repairs. Drop the car off if needed; we provide a loaner. You're covered.

**"Will passengers judge me for driving a Tesla?"**
Opposite. Most passengers love it. Many ask questions. Be ready to explain the benefits. It's a great conversation starter.

**"Can I drive to LA or San Diego?"**
Yes, within your rental area. Just plan charging stops on longer trips. Tesla's navigation handles this automatically.

**"What's the cancellation policy?"**
You can return the car anytime. No long-term contract required. Mongoori prefers stability but understands if it's not for you.

## Week 2+: Optimizing Your Earnings

Once you're comfortable with the Tesla and familiar with Irvine's rideshare zones, it's time to optimize:

### 1. Identify Your Peak Times
Track which days/times you earn the most. For most Irvine drivers:
- Friday & Saturday 9 PM-1 AM (surge multipliers 1.5-2.5x)
- Weekday mornings 7-9 AM (1.2-1.5x)
- Weekday 5-7 PM (1.2-1.4x)

**Week 2 goal:** Shift 50% of your hours to surge times.

### 2. Master One High-Value Zone
Don't drive all over Irvine randomly. Pick one zone and become an expert:
- Irvine Spectrum (lots of nightlife, good tips)
- Turtle Ridge residential (upscale passengers, better tippers)
- Business parks (morning/afternoon, quick rides)

Knowing every street, restaurant, and hotel in one zone makes you faster and more confident.

### 3. Maintain Your Rating Above 4.85
After your first 50 rides, you should be 4.8+. Get to 4.85+ and stay there:
- Clean car every day
- Friendly conversation (not forced)
- Smooth driving
- Cold water and phone chargers available
- Follow routes precisely

Higher rating = better ride acceptance = more earnings.

### 4. Track Three Numbers Weekly
- **Total earnings:** Gross from Uber/Lyft
- **Rental cost deducted:** What Mongoori takes (usually $400-530)
- **Net profit:** What you actually take home

Most Mongoori drivers hit $2,500-3,500/month net after 6-8 weeks of optimization. You're not there yet, and that's fine—you're building the skills.

## Common Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: "The App Feels Overwhelming"

**Solution:**
- Uber/Lyft apps are simpler than they look
- Stick to 2-3 familiar zones the first two weeks
- Watch a YouTube tutorial on driver basics (5 minutes)
- Call Mongoori support if you're confused (they're friendly)

### Challenge 2: "I'm Getting Low Ratings for Navigation"

**Solution:**
- Use Google Maps instead of Tesla's navigation—it's better for driving
- Tell passengers the route before starting ("I'm taking Santa Clara to Spectrum, then back to your place")
- Avoid weird routes; passengers notice
- If you get a bad rating, ask yourself what went wrong and fix it next time

### Challenge 3: "I Don't Know Where to Charge"

**Solution:**
- Download Plugshare and ChargePoint
- Screenshot 3-4 free charging spots near your main zones
- Most Tesla drivers never need public charging (home charging is enough)
- Plan one longer trip with public charging to get comfortable

### Challenge 4: "The Rental Payment Feels Too High"

**Solution:**
- Remember: gas car costs $400-550/month too
- But you also save $450-500/month in fuel + $150-200 in maintenance
- Net advantage: $400-700/month even accounting for rental costs
- If you're not seeing ROI, you're not optimizing your hours
